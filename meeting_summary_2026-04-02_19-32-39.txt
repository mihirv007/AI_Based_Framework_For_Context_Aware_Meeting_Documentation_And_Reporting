*   **Project Financials:**

    *   **Overall Profit Aim:** 50 million Euro.
    *   **Maximum Production Cost:** 12.50 Euro per unit (to achieve profit target).
    *   **Market Range:** International, specifically for television.
*   **Remote Control User Experience Brainstorming:**
    *   **Common Frustrations:**
        *   Too many buttons/steps to turn on a TV.
        *   "Multi-media overload" on combined remotes.
        *   Difficulty locating remotes.
        *   Remotes requiring specific aiming/movement for infrared to work.
        *   Too many separate remotes for different devices.
        *   Small, hard-to-press buttons.
    *   **Desired Features/Improvements:**
        *   Simplicity and ease of use.
        *   Clear, well-defined button functions.
        *   Larger, more accessible buttons.
        *   Effective and reliable infrared (or alternative) functionality.
        *   Long-lasting batteries (e.g., lithium).
        *   Microphone functionality.
        *   A potential solution for the "too many buttons" vs. "too many remotes" dilemma: a primary remote for core functions (on/off, channel, volume) and a separate remote for less frequently used, specialized functions.
        *   Spatially divided buttons on a single remote for different device functions (if combined).
